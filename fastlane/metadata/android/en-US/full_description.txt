Zero-Trust Keyboard keeps what you type on your phone. Word suggestions, autocorrect, swipe typing, voice typing and translation all run on the device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

WHAT IT DOES
• Hebrew and English side by side, including both in one sentence, with a Hebrew dictionary of more than 180,000 words.
• Swipe typing in every language that has a dictionary, with our own open decoder.
• Voice typing on the phone: the sound is turned into text on the device and is never stored or sent. Includes a model tuned for Hebrew.
• Translate what you wrote with one key, on the phone, between the languages you choose.
• Emoji search and emoji suggestions in Hebrew and English: type dog or כלב and the emoji is offered.
• Clipboard history with pinned clips.
• Personal dictionary and shortcuts, and suggestions that learn from the way you write.
• One-handed, split and floating keyboard, a number row and a toolbar you arrange yourself.
• Themes, key borders, your own background image and custom layouts.
• Incognito mode: nothing is learned while it is on.

WHY ZERO-TRUST
• What you type never leaves the phone. The internet is used only to download a voice or translation model you ask for, and for the optional account.
• Optional end-to-end encrypted sync of your personal dictionary, learned words, pinned clips and settings through your Zero-Trust account. Each one is off until you turn it on. We can't read them, and neither can anyone who breaks into the server.
• Works with no account at all.
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics, no data sold. Ever.
• Open source (AGPL-3.0), so anyone can check what the keyboard does. Built on HeliBoard.
• English and Hebrew, with full right-to-left support.

Part of the Zero-Trust suite: Notes, Tasks, Calendar, Contacts, Phone, Fitness, Meds, Time, Browser and more. One account opens them all.
